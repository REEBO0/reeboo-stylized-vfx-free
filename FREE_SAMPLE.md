# REEBOO Stylized VFX - Free Sample

Three effects from the full pack (Hit Spark, Slash and Pickup Sparkle), released under the MIT
license (see LICENSE).

The full pack adds 22 more: explosion, fireball, fire impact, lightning strike, heal, level up,
shield, portal, poison cloud, ice burst, muzzle flash, ground slam, campfire, burning, smoke poof,
water splash, arcane missile, collect burst, shadow burst, teleport, power aura and electric hit.
Get it at https://reeboo-labs.itch.io/stylized-vfx-godot

Open this folder in Godot 4.4+ and press F5 to see the effects. Usage notes are in README.md.
